A Houston-based technology provider for local government, education and small businesses. We source what you need, set it up properly and stay reachable afterward.

Our Mission

We started IT Prescription to give local organizations the kind of technology partner larger enterprises take for granted: one that listens first, prescribes what fits, and stays accountable after the sale.

That means plain-language advice, quotes you can follow line by line, and a team that answers the phone when something isn't working.
